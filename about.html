Hi, I'm Omar Alali. I'm from Saudi Arabia and a Cybersecurity graduate from Florida International University (FIU) with a 3.97 / 4.00 GPA. I also earned an Undergraduate Certificate in Ethics, Big Data, and Artificial Intelligence.

My journey into technology began in an unexpected way. I originally studied Business for two years, but over time I realized my true passion was technology. While studying business, I wanted a gaming PC but couldn't afford a prebuilt one. Instead, I bought the individual parts and taught myself how to build it by watching YouTube videos and reading manuals. Successfully building that computer sparked my interest in technology and inspired me to pursue a completely different career.

After making that decision, I moved to the United States to learn English before beginning my bachelor's degree in Cybersecurity at Florida International University.

Today, I'm passionate about cybersecurity, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, networking, automation, and emerging technologies. I enjoy building hands-on projects, continuously learning new skills, and earning industry certifications while exploring different areas of technology and security.

My goal is to become a well-rounded technology professional with the flexibility to work across cybersecurity, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, networking, automation, software development, and other emerging technologies — continuing to learn, build, and make a meaningful impact wherever I go.
